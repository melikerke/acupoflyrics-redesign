||| my whole life's gone to waste: The Turkish phrase “Bütün hayatım ziyan oldu” is especially forceful. It conveys not only lost time, but also wasted potential, effort, and hope. “Gone to waste” preserves that sense of helplessness.
||| burn on your own: “Burn” is figurative here: to suffer and wear oneself down. The line is a harsh way of saying, “I cannot help you or offer advice; you must face your pain alone.”

||| knives in my back: The image suggests so many betrayals—being stabbed in the back—that the narrator has become numb to the pain and begun to treat it as normal.
||| hit rock bottom: “Hit rock bottom” is the idiomatic English equivalent of “diplere kavuşmak”: reaching the lowest possible point in one's life.
||| the bottom is mine: This is one of the song's strongest contrasts. The narrator has embraced rock bottom so fully that it now feels like his own place, while the parenthetical recalls how hopeful and dream-driven he once was.
||| Even my own body rejects me: This line expresses an extreme form of self-loathing: the narrator feels so alienated from himself that even his body seems to cast him out.
